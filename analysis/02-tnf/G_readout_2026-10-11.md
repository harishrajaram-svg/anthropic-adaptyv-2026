## G1 and G4: the attribution is real, and it is position-specific

Parents were inverted in 5 of 5 seeds (L49H median **2.1066**, quad **1.7935**). Neutralising the
named sites corrects the direction in both, replicated:

- `L49H_E4Q` seeds: 0.963, 0.924, 0.854, 0.756, 0.823 — **below 1.0 in 5 of 5**
- `quadH_E3Q` seeds: 0.947, 0.961, 1.064, 0.785, 0.790 — below 1.0 in 4 of 5

**The control settles that this is not generic.** `quadH_CTRL3Q` neutralises three *non*-contributing
glutamates, count-matched, and lands at median **1.4968, 0 of 5 below 1.0**. The binder-owned column
is the cleanest read: both targeted variants fall to exactly **1.0000** — no binder site contributes
at all any more — while the control sits at **1.9073**. The gate's per-molecule attribution picked
the right residues, and three arbitrary glutamates do not substitute for them.

This is the first time in this project that the predicted pH direction has been **deliberately
corrected and replicated across seeds**, with a matched control excluding the generic explanation.

## G2: and the same glutamates carry the binding

| | margin | change from parent |
|---|--:|--:|
| `quadH` (parent) | +7.1306 | — |
| `quadH_CTRL3Q` (3 random Glu) | +6.8301 | **−0.30** |
| `quadH_E3Q` (3 attributed Glu) | +6.2248 | **−0.91** |
| `L49H_E4Q` (4 attributed Glu) | +6.3348 | fails by **0.0125** |

Removing three arbitrary glutamates costs 0.30 of margin; removing the three the gate blames costs
**0.91**. The attributed residues are *more* load-bearing than random ones, which is the double bind:
the sites that invert the pH response are the sites that hold the complex. `L49H_E4Q` misses the bar
by 0.0125 and `quadH_E3Q` by 0.1225 — both reported as failures, not rounded.

This is the failure mode the pre-registration named in advance ("G2 is the bar most likely to fail…
A3 knocked out His61 and the margin fell from +8.031 to +0.916").

## 🔴 My stated prediction was wrong, and in an informative direction

The pre-registration predicted **≈0.70** for L49H and **≈0.73** for the quad, as the product of the
two remaining helping sites. Observed: **0.8538** and **0.9468**, both weaker. Target-owned also
moved — 0.8443 and 0.8593 against the predicted 0.701/0.732 — so **removing binder carboxylates
changed the target's own pKa shifts**. The sites are coupled, not independent, and the naive product
over-promised. Recorded because the prereg said it would be.

## What this licenses next

Deleting the glutamates is the wrong operation: it fixes the direction and costs the hold. The right
one is to **rebuild the hold elsewhere on the binder face while keeping those positions
non-titratable** — which is what SolubleMPNN on the predicted complex does, and it is now possible
because full-atom predictions exist (CA-only ProteinMPNN cannot use soluble weights). G2's deficits
are 0.0125 and 0.1225; a redesign has to recover a tenth of a margin unit, not a whole one.

ESMFold2 is pH-agnostic. No outcome here demonstrates a switch, and objective 2 stays reported as
not demonstrated.
