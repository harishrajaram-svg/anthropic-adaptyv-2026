RESULT, and it is the first pH result in this project that is not purely negative:

B3 PASSES 5 of 6, three of them at 5 of 5 seeds. The releasing geometry is STRUCTURAL and
REPRODUCIBLE. It did not have to be forced -- ProteinMPNN placed every one of these histidines of
its own accord -- and unlike C4's target-histidine geometry (2 of 5 at best) it survives reseeding.
Two of the 5/5 designs point at R32, which is CONSERVED IN MOUSE, so the arrangement is compatible
with objective 3 as well.

B1 FAILS 0 of 6. Best margin +5.051 against a floor of +6.347.

=> THE GEOMETRY IS NOT THE PROBLEM. THE BINDING IS.
The mechanism is installable without forcing and stable across seeds; what does not happen is a
design that holds TNF-alpha while carrying it. Across every design in this project with a measured
margin, releasing geometry never exceeds ~+5.1 (C6 best +5.051; the only previously measured
releasing-His design sits near +5.1) while the best design without it reaches +8.322.

The confound to check was "releasing-His designs are simply less optimised." It does not hold:
lineage L2 on backbone 3 carried its His28 releasing geometry through rounds 5, 8 and NINE of
margin-driven redesign and plateaued at +5.051, while lineages that never had one or lost it
reached +8.322 in four to five rounds. Nine rounds of optimisation did not convert the geometry
into a hold.

Read with C3 (the margin is interface-localised, and Arg27 carries it), the picture is geometric:
on this target the residues that carry binding and the positions where a protonatable histidine
would push the interface apart are not the same places. That is a statement about TNF-alpha's
inter-protomer site, not about our pipeline, and it is the best explanation this project has for
why objective 2 is hard here.

DECLARED, unchanged: ESMFold2 is pH-agnostic. B3 is a geometric arrangement in a predicted pose,
not a pH measurement, and no histidine pKa was predicted -- PROPKA was retired on this project
because its within-design noise exceeded its between-design signal. Nothing here is a demonstrated
pH switch.
