RESULT 1 -- pH: NEGATIVE, and decisively so. 0 of 4 clear B3. Per the prereg, a pH slot needed
B1 AND B3, so no candidate qualifies. The single-seed release geometry did not survive reseeding:
3_L0r2k2, whose seed-42 contact was the cleanest in the pool (4.07 A cation vs 8.04 A carboxylate),
reads no-contact in 5 of 5 fresh seeds. This is a stronger negative than the project's three earlier
pH closures, because it is not an instrument failing its control -- the geometry itself is a
single-seed artifact, caught by a bar fixed before the folds existed.
=> Objective 2 is closed for the fourth time. Mechanism B joins mechanism A as measured dead.

RESULT 2 -- an unexpected binder, and it needs the same scepticism C2 just taught us.
40_L3r5k2 clears BOTH margin bars at +8.143, higher than 40_L1r4k1's own 5-seed +8.031 and higher
than its pooled 15-seed +7.706. Three caveats, stated rather than buried:
  (a) It is ONE 5-seed draw. C2 measured a -0.374 shift between 5-seed and fresh-seed reads on the
      leader, so +8.143 may deflate the same way. Seeds 6-15 are the test, not more argument.
  (b) On the DESIGN side alone it is worse: pae_if_mean 7.805 against the leader's 6.997 (lower is
      better). Its larger margin comes partly from a worse null (15.947 vs 15.027). The margin is
      the pre-registered instrument because the 150-design calibration selected it, and both
      molecules are 83-mers, but the raw interface PAE ranks them the other way and that is reported.
  (c) It carries 1 histidine and has never been through express_qc_p2, the prior-art prescreen, the
      monomer floor, or a mouse leg. None of that is known.
It comes out of the v3 arm's discarded human-side tree -- the designs the worse-of-two selector
built and then threw away. That lesson now has a number attached.

ADDENDUM, 1:06 AM -- mechanism B was objective-3 dead anyway, found while staging C5.
The mouse construct's histidines sit at 15, 20, 77; the human construct's at 15, 73, 78. The ONLY
interface-accessible human target histidine, H73, has no mouse counterpart at the equivalent
position. So even had the geometry replicated, a target-histidine switch would have been
human-only, against a spec that requires mouse cross-reactivity. The negative is therefore
over-determined: the geometry is a single-seed artifact AND the residue it depends on is not
conserved in the second required species.
