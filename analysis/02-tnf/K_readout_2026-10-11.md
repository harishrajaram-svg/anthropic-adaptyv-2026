## 🔴 Where it sits against what already ships, and the two caveats

Worse-of-two: `40_L1r4k1` **+7.207**, `3_L0r4k0_quadH` +7.131, `3_L0r4k0` +6.967,
**`quadH_E3Q_k12` +6.747.** So it is the **weakest cross-species minimum** of the candidates while
being the only one with replicated direction evidence. It adds rather than displaces, so that is a
"should it go in", not a "what does it replace".

**1. Zero histidines.** Its predicted direction comes entirely from target His73 and Glu146. It
cannot switch by the intended mechanism and inherits the caveat already carried for `3_L4r5k2`.

**2. The instrument is uncalibrated and does not discriminate** — 5.8% of our designs clear the gate
against 5.6% of composition-matched shuffles. Five-of-five means **reproducible, not real.** R1 is
what would change that, and the literature so far suggests it will land on its underpowered-pilot
fallback.
