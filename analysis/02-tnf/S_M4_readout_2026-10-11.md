## The lead worth following

`tnfa_seam_s3_22k4` is the closest this project has come: **three H73 engaged, a real pose (0.5936,
lrms 2.868 Å), and target-owned ratio 0.7573 — the target side genuinely helping.** Its total is
1.0086 only because its **binder** side contributes 1.2364. Neutralise that and it sits near 0.76.

And there is a principled way to try: the binder side is exactly what **SolubleMPNN on the predicted
complex** redesigns, which is the step this arm had to skip. CA-only ProteinMPNN cannot use soluble
weights, so stage 2 ran vanilla CA weights on the backbone; now that a prediction exists, the
established depth-loop redesign applies to it normally. That is a second round, not a new mechanism.
