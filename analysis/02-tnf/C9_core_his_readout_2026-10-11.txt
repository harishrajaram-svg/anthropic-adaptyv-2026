WHY THIS WORKED WHERE SEVEN ROUTES FAILED: the histidine is not at the interface, so it does not
compete with the residues that carry the binding. C3 measured that competition directly (the margin
is carried by Arg27, Thr54 and Tyr20, none titratable; six distal alanines moved it 0.198-0.569),
and C9 is that measurement used as a design principle rather than as a diagnosis.

WHAT IS CLAIMED: a matched pair differing by one buried histidine, where the variant binds at least
as well as the parent and folds as well.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, and this is the part a reviewer must not be allowed to misread:
  - NO pH switch is demonstrated. ESMFold2 is pH-agnostic; no pKa was computed; PROPKA was retired
    on this project because its within-design noise exceeded its between-design signal.
  - The predicted mechanism is FOLD DESTABILISATION at pH 6.0 -- a desolvated imidazolium with no
    polar partner -- argued from established biophysics (influenza HA, diphtheria T-domain), NOT
    from anything we ran.
  - That mechanism's failure mode at pH 6.0 is LOSS OF STRUCTURE, which the assay cannot distinguish
    from aggregation. We predict unfolding, not a clean electrostatic release, and the methods
    document says so outright.
  - The magnitude is unknown: burial in an 83-residue bundle may not shift the pKa enough to titrate
    between 7.4 and 6.0.
